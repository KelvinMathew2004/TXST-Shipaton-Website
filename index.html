Code of Conduct

TXST Shipaton is dedicated to providing a safe, inclusive, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior in any form. All attendees, sponsors, partners, and volunteers are required to comply with this code of conduct throughout the entirety of the event.

If you experience or witness any unacceptable behavior, please immediately reach out to a judge or event organizer. Violations may result in immediate expulsion from the hackathon at the sole discretion of the organizers.
